What the numbers mean
Refereed papers
Peer-reviewed journal papers, as listed by NASA SciX (successor of NASA ADS), the standard bibliographic database of astronomy.
Citations
How many times these papers have been cited by other papers indexed in SciX.
h-index
The largest number h such that h papers have each been cited at least h times. Proposed by J. E. Hirsch (2005, PNAS 102, 16569) as a single number combining productivity and impact.
i10-index
The number of papers cited at least 10 times; introduced by Google Scholar in 2011.
First / corresponding author
Papers led by our group: Jae-Young Kim as first author, or as corresponding author of papers written with a student.
