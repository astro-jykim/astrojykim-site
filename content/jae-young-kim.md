## Specific research interests

- Science cases | Supermassive black holes (SMBHs), accretion and outflow physics in compact objects, radio astronomy, high energy astrophysics, multi-wavelength and multi-messenger astrophysics, co-evolution between SMBHs and galaxies, tests of general relativity, 21cm cosmology

- Methods (instrumentation & algorithms) | Interferometry, interferometric imaging and analysis algorithms, broadband polarimetry and spectroscopy, high dynamic range imaging, timeseries and broadband SED modeling, machine learning and deep learning techniques, millimeter/meter-wavelength instrumentation, application of advanced computing modules to astronomy (e.g., FPGA)
